ABOUT

Affordable prints, made with care.

Smith3DPrintingCo is built around straightforward pricing and small-batch 3D printing. The goal is simple: make interesting display pieces without overcharging for the print.

Materials & replica notice

Products in this collection are printed from PETG plastic. They are inert, non-functional display replicas and are not ammunition. They contain no primer, powder, propellant, or firing mechanism.

Because these products are made to order, contact us before returning an item. If an item arrives damaged, defective, or materially different from the ordered configuration, email us within 7 days of delivery with your order information and photos.
